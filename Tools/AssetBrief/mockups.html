The prompt

The same text is in Tools/AssetBrief/README.md in the addon folder.

You are drawing the optional art pack for AzUI Studio, the options window of the World of Warcraft addon AzUI Color Picker. The window is already built and works without this pack. The pack adds hand-made art where the window currently uses generated shapes or nothing at all.

Read first
- Tools/AssetBrief/mockups.html: every asset in place, at real size, with its slot and file name.
- Options/StudioPack.lua: the list of files and the switches that turn each piece on.
- Options/Studio.lua: the palette (Studio.C) and the generated shapes the art must sit beside (Media/Studio/*.tga, made by Tools/MakeStudioArt.py).

Look
Flat and dark, with rounded surfaces, a thin bronze window edge, and gold for whatever is selected. Titles are set in Marcellus, everything else in Source Sans 3. The art should feel like the bronze and gold fittings of a well-made instrument case: fine engraved lines and restrained ornament, not glossy or painterly. It should echo the spirit of AzeriteUI. AzUI has two looks: AzUI Studio, its own design, which this pack is for, and AzUI Azerite, which already dresses the same window in AzeriteUI's own art, read at runtime from the installed AzeriteUI. Blakmane and Goldpaw, AzeriteUI's designer and developer, have given permission to use their art, but never copy AzeriteUI's files into AzUI: reference them from AzeriteUI's folder instead. This pack is AzUI's own, so draw it new; matching AzeriteUI's bronze-and-stone feel is welcome.

Palette: window #15141B, surface #1D1C24, raised #2B2733, line #2E2B36, bronze #6E5B2A, gold #FFD100, goldDim #5A4513, text #E8E2D0, muted #9C95A8.

Files to make (all 32-bit TGA with alpha, into Media/StudioPack/)
1. logo.tga, 64x64. The mark left of the "AzUI Studio" title (drawn at 28x28). A small emblem: a health-bar sliver crossing a gem or prism, gold and bronze on transparent. It must read at 28 px: two or three shapes at most.
2. corner.tga, 64x64. Bronze filigree for the top-left corner of the window, drawn at 40x40 and mirrored by code for the other three. The outer curve follows the window's 10 px corner radius, 3 px outside it. Bronze #6E5B2A, highlights no brighter than #A08A4E. Keep the inner two thirds transparent.
3. condition-<key>.tga, 64x64 each, white on transparent (the code tints them gold): combat (crossed blades), mounted (horse head or saddle), resting (moon over an inn roof), stealth (eye with a slash), dead (skull, or a soft ghost), spec (three stacked talent diamonds), instance (stone doorway). Drawn at 20x20: 2 px strokes at that size (about 6 px at 64), one consistent line style, no fine detail.
4. empty-rules.tga, 256x128. Above "No rules yet" on the Rules page: a quiet line illustration of a health bar changing colour as it passes a gate or banner. Gold and muted lines on transparent, no background box.
5. empty-pets.tga, 256x128. Above "No pets known yet" on the Hunter Pets page: an empty stable stall with a paw print, in the same style.

Then
- Set each finished piece to true in Studio.Pack in Options/StudioPack.lua.
- Run the tests from the addon folder with Lua 5.1; all must pass: lua Tools/TestStudio.lua, lua Tools/TestPanel.lua, lua Tools/TestPanel.lua art, lua Tools/TestPanel.lua plain, lua Tools/TestFeatures.lua, lua Tools/TestClients.lua.
- Add a short note under ## [Unreleased] in changelog.md naming the art you added.
- Put the source files you drew from (SVG or layered files) in Tools/AssetBrief/source/. Tools/ is not packaged.

Checks before you finish
- Each TGA opens with alpha and has the exact size above.
- condition-*.tga are pure white where opaque, so tinting gives a clean gold.
- Look at each piece at the size it is drawn in game (28, 40, 20 and 256x128 px) on #15141B; it must still read.
